Online Workshop Industry & Academic Bridging
Feb 3, 2026

Empowering Future Engineers with Practical Project Management in Software Industry

Software Project Module — 24th Batch (3rd Year Undergraduates), University of Ruhuna

Invited by Ms. Yugani Gamlath to conduct a specialized online workshop for 3rd-year engineering undergraduates. The session focused on bridging academic engineering projects with real-world software industry practices, emphasizing how structured project management directly drives successful software delivery.

Workshop Modules & Technical Coverage:
  • • Project Fundamentals & The Iron Triangle: Practical tradeoffs across Scope, Time, and Cost in software delivery.
  • • Agile & Scrum Methodologies: Iterative delivery principles, Scrum team roles, artifacts, and sprint ceremonies.
  • • Hands-On Jira Implementation: Live demonstration mapping Epics, User Stories, Tasks, Bugs, Story Points, and active Sprint boards to enforce team accountability.
