My Contribution

Movement Tracking & Scoring Mechanism

As identified in the project responsibility matrix, I led the design and implementation of the computer vision–based hand tracking and exercise scoring subsystem — the core evaluation engine of the mobile rehabilitation component.

01

Markerless Hand Tracking

Integrated MediaPipe Hand Landmark detection to track hand movements without any wearable markers using the device camera.

02

Landmark Extraction

Extracted all 21 MediaPipe hand landmarks per frame, capturing fine-grained finger and wrist joint positions.

03

Landmark Normalization

Normalized landmark coordinates to eliminate camera distance and orientation bias, ensuring consistent scoring.

04

Movement Analysis

Analysed multi-joint kinematics across frames to characterize movement quality and range of motion.

05

Exercise-Specific Scoring

Designed separate scoring algorithms for each rehabilitation exercise: pinch grip, cylindrical grip, spherical grip, and wrist rotation.

06

Performance Evaluation & Fine-Tuning

Tested scoring against clinician benchmarks, iteratively tuned thresholds, and validated accuracy against clinical ground truth.

Targeted Hand Functions

The system targets three core functional hand activities across eight exercise levels covering different movement conditions and difficulty gradations.

✍️

Pinch / Pen Grip

Precision pinch movements targeting fine motor control essential for daily writing and manipulation tasks.

🍼

Cylindrical Grip

Whole-hand power grip exercises simulating holding cylindrical objects such as cups or bottles.

🏀

Spherical Grip

Spherical grasp exercises targeting the coordination required to hold ball-shaped or rounded objects.

🔄

Wrist Rotation

Pronation and supination exercises to restore range of motion and coordination in the wrist joint.
