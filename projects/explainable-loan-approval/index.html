Comparative Analysis of Intrinsic Glass-Box Models and Post-Hoc XAI Frameworks for Explainable Loan Approval Prediction

A systematic comparative evaluation of intrinsic glass-box architectures (GAM, EBM, KAN) against 12 traditional linear and black-box gradient boosting classifiers on a 52,000-record credit dataset, establishing glass-box models as state-of-the-art solutions for transparent, audit-compliant financial AI.

Overview

In financial underwriting and automated credit risk scoring, opaque black-box machine learning models introduce severe regulatory and compliance risks under GDPR Article 22, FCRA, and ECOA adverse action mandates. While post-hoc explainability methods like SHAP and LIME attempt to approximate black-box behavior, they suffer from sampling instability, computational overhead, and surrogate fidelity trade-offs. This study presents a rigorous comparative benchmark of intrinsic glass-box architectures—Generalized Additive Models (GAM), Explainable Boosting Machines (EBM), and Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks (KAN)—demonstrating that glass-box models achieve predictive parity with top-performing gradient boosting ensembles while providing exact, unapproximated feature explanations.

Key Contributions & Findings

  • Predictive Parity Without Accuracy Trade-Offs — Intrinsic glass-box models (LogisticGAM and ExplainableBoostingClassifier) achieve 85.11% test accuracy, 88.91% positive F1-score, and 0.8238 / 0.8218 ROC-AUC, matching top black-box ensembles (LightGBM 85.11%, CatBoost 85.11%).
  • Exact Additive Log-Odds Attribution — Eliminates post-hoc sampling errors by generating exact, unapproximated additive feature shape functions and local score decompositions (logit(P) = β₀ + ∑ Scoreⱼ), directly fulfilling legal adverse action notice requirements.
  • High Computational Efficiency — EBM trains in 2.80 seconds, outperforming traditional gradient boosting frameworks by 20×–40× in training speed while eliminating runtime post-hoc explanation overhead.
  • Diagnostic Evaluation of Tabular KANs — Uncovered optimization instability in edge-based continuous B-spline KANs on un-binned financial tabular data compared to tree-binned EBMs, providing key architectural insights for tabular deep learning.
  • Fair Lending & Bias Auditing — Verified that financial risk features (Credit Score, Annual Income, Outstanding Debt) account for over 85% of total predictive weight, preventing demographic proxy bias in automated credit allocation.
